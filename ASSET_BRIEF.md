## Things I need you to source

For all of these: **royalty-free with a commercial-use license** (Unsplash and Pexels are free and licensed for this; if you use anything from a stock marketplace like Artgrid/Envato/Shutterstock, keep the license/receipt — this site takes payments, so "commercial use" actually matters here, not just a formality).

### 1. Hero background — mood/atmosphere layer
Sits *behind* the laptop mockup, dark and cinematic, similar role to Provet's gradient-over-video treatment.
- **Subject:** something evoking security operations / engineering at night — a server room with status LEDs, a dark data-center aisle, a SOC-style multi-monitor setup, or an abstract dark network/particle visualization. Not literal photos of people (this site deliberately keeps the person anonymous — see the pixelated profile photo).
- **Mood:** dark, moody, blue/teal-tinted or neutral — not bright/corporate.
- **Format:** either a **video** (MP4, H.264, 1920×1080 minimum, 10–20 second seamless loop, muted, no sudden motion — needs to sit calmly behind foreground content) or a **static image** at **3840×2160 (4K)**.
- **If it's a video and you want the scroll-scrub effect** (video frame tied to scroll position, like Provet's laptop-zoom): give me something with a clear beginning → end visual progression over its duration (e.g., camera slowly pushing in, or lights sequentially activating) so scrubbing through it on scroll actually reads as intentional, not random.

### 2. Section background imagery (2–4 images)
Used behind value-prop / feature sections, same role as Provet's "vet with client" photography — but our domain is engineering/security, not veterinary care.
- **Subject options:** code on a dark monitor (shallow depth of field), a security operations center, server racks / data center corridor, a close-up of a keyboard with dramatic lighting, abstract circuit/network patterns.
- **Format:** 3840×2160 (4K), landscape orientation.
- **Mood:** same as above — moody, professional, not stock-photo-cheesy (avoid the "person smiling at a laptop" cliché shots).

### 3. Optional — testimonial-adjacent or trust-signal imagery
Provet uses client logos and headshots here. We don't have real client testimonials to fabricate, so I'm skipping this section rather than faking it — no sourcing needed unless you want to add something specific later.
